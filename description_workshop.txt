# Gilded Blade Wings — Growth Wings, Tesseract Summons & Anime Vanities

A Terraria tModLoader mod featuring growth wings, a tesseract summon weapon, an automatic miner, bulk extractinator support, and a built-in anime texture pack with selectable vanity outfits.

## Features

### Gilded Blade Wings
- Wings grow stronger as you defeat bosses
- Each boss defeat increases flight time by 0.5 seconds
- Horizontal flight speed improves with each boss defeated
- Additional stat boosts per boss:
  - +5 Max HP
  - +20% Damage
  - +0.5% Damage Reduction
  - +1 Defense
  - +6 Life Regeneration
  - +5 Max Mana
- Every 5 boss defeats:
  - +1 Minion Slot
  - +1 Sentinel Slot

### Tesseract Staff
- Summons up to 10 shadow copies that attack using items from your inventory
- Each summon uses 2 minion slots, maximum 10 simultaneous summons
- Copies use the 10 inventory slots along the bottom row
- Projectiles fire from the copy's position, not the player
- Unique cube rendering with configurable parameters:
  - Glow intensity
  - Line thickness
  - Color saturation
  - Animation speed
  - Toggle planet texture, wings, and weapon display
- Damage displays as "???", actual damage depends on the item used by each copy

### Azafure Digital Miner
- Automatically mines ores from the world
- 6 filter slots to specify which ore types to mine
- 35 item storage slots
- Supports gem mining (Amethyst, Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond)
- Whitelist feature to add custom ores or items
- Tile and inventory data stay in sync in multiplayer

### Bulk Extractinator
- Right-click an Extractinator or Chlorophyte Extractinator to open the extraction UI
- Processes a whole stack of extractable items at once, sending results straight to your inventory and Void Vault
- Toggle between stopping when your inventory is full and extracting everything regardless of space
- Chlorophyte Extractinator supports bulk ore conversion
- Hold Shift and click to quickly move extractable items into the extraction slot

### Anime Texture Pack & Vanities
- Client-side replacement of player body parts, hairstyles, wings, back accessories, extra effects, glow masks, and projectiles
- 5 vanity sets (15 pieces total): Lily Asuka, Khoir Bach, Rias Gremory, Ram, and Rem
- Every vanity piece is crafted at a Loom with 5 Silk
- Vanity Wardrobe: new characters start with one. Use it to open the selection UI, claim a full set, and the wardrobe is consumed

### Configuration
- Adjustable tesseract rendering: glow, lines, colors, animation speed
- Toggle planet texture, wings, and weapon display
- Miner whitelist for additional ore items
- Low-fidelity mode for performance optimization

## How to Obtain

### Gilded Blade Wings
Craft at a Workbench with:
- 20 Iron Bars
- 20 Fallen Stars

### Tesseract Staff
Craft at a Workbench with:
- 1 Finch Staff

### Azafure Miner
Craft at a Heavy Work Bench with:
- 10 Iron Bars

### Vanities
Craft each piece at a Loom with:
- 5 Silk

### Vanity Wardrobe
Given to newly created characters automatically.

## Requirement

- InnoVault

# 耀刃之翼 —— 成长型翅膀、超立方召唤与动漫时装

一个包含成长型翅膀、超立方召唤物、自动采矿机、批量提取，以及内置动漫材质包与可选时装的 Terraria tModLoader 模组。

## 主要特性

### 耀刃之翼成长系统
- 翅膀会随着你击败 BOSS 而变得更强大
- 每击败一个 BOSS，飞行时间增加 0.5 秒
- 水平飞行速度随击败 BOSS 数量提升
- 每个 BOSS 提供的额外属性加成：
  - +5 最大生命值
  - +20% 伤害
  - +0.5% 伤害减免
  - +1 防御力
  - +6 生命再生
  - +5 最大魔力
- 每击败 5 个 BOSS：
  - +1 召唤栏
  - +1 哨兵栏

### 超立方杖
- 召唤最多 10 个虚空分身使用背包物品攻击敌人
- 每个分身占用 2 个召唤栏，最多同时存在 10 个
- 分身使用背包底部一排的 10 个槽位
- 弹幕从分身位置发射，而非玩家位置
- 独特的立方体线条渲染，支持配置：
  - 散光强度
  - 线条粗细
  - 颜色饱和度
  - 动画速度
  - 可开关星球纹理、翅膀、武器显示
- 攻击力显示为“???”，实际伤害取决于槽位中的物品

### 阿萨福勒数字型采矿机
- 自动挖掘世界中的矿石
- 6 个过滤器槽位用于设置要挖掘的矿石类型
- 35 个物品存储槽位
- 支持宝石挖掘（紫宝石、黄宝石、蓝宝石、绿宝石、红宝石、钻石）
- 白名单功能可添加额外的矿石或物品
- 多人游戏中方块与库存数据保持同步

### 批量提取
- 右键提取机或叶绿提取机打开提取界面
- 一次处理整组可提取物，产物自动进入背包与虚空袋
- 可切换“背包空间不足时停止”与“无视背包剩余空间全部提取”
- 叶绿提取机支持批量矿物互换
- 按住 Shift 点击可将可提取物快速放入提取槽

### 动漫材质包与时装
- 客户端替换玩家体型、发型、翅膀、背后饰品、Extra 光效、发光遮罩与弹幕外观
- 5 套独立时装（共 15 件）：莉莉·阿斯卡、科尔·巴赫、莉雅丝·吉蒙里、拉姆、蕾姆
- 每件时装在织布机处使用 5 个丝绸合成
- 时装衣橱：新建角色初始携带。使用后打开选择界面，领取整套时装后衣橱消失

### 配置
- 超立方渲染参数配置：散光、线条、饱和度、动画速度
- 可开关星球纹理、翅膀、武器显示
- 采矿机白名单物品设置
- 低精度模式优化性能

## 获取方式

### 耀刃之翼
在工作台合成：
- 20 个铁锭
- 20 个落星

### 超立方杖
在工作台合成：
- 1 根雀杖（Finch Staff）

### 阿萨福勒采矿机
在重型工作台合成：
- 10 个铁锭

### 时装
在织布机合成：
- 每件 5 个丝绸

### 时装衣橱
新建角色时自动放入物品栏。
