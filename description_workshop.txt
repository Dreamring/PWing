# Gilded Blade Wings & Tesseract Staff

A wings mod with growth mechanics, a tesseract summon weapon, and an auto-miner for Terraria.

## Key Features

### Gilded Blade Wings Growth System
- Wings grow stronger as you defeat bosses
- Each boss defeat increases flight time by 0.5 seconds
- Horizontal flight speed improves with each boss defeated
- Additional stat boosts per boss:
  - +5 Max HP
  - +20% Damage
  - +0.5% Damage Reduction
  - +1 Defense
  - +6 Life Regeneration
  - +5 Max Mana
- Every 5 boss defeats:
  - +1 Minion Slot
  - +1 Sentinel Slot

### Tesseract Staff (超立方杖)
- Summons up to 10 shadow copies that attack using items from your inventory
- Each summon uses 2 minion slots, maximum 10 simultaneous summons
- Copies use items from inventory slots 40-49
- Projectiles fire from the copy's position, not the player
- Unique cube rendering with configurable parameters:
  - Glow intensity
  - Line thickness
  - Color saturation
  - Animation speed
  - Toggle planet texture, wings, and weapon display
- Damage displays as "???", actual damage depends on the equipped item in each slot
- Crafted with Finch Staff at a Workbench

### Azafure Digital Miner
- Automatically mines ores from the world
- 6 filter slots to specify which ore types to mine
- 35 item storage slots
- Supports gem mining (Amethyst, Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond)
- Whitelist feature to add custom ores or items
- Visual feedback with particle effects
- Tile and inventory data stay in sync in multiplayer

### Configuration
- Adjustable tesseract rendering: glow, lines, colors, animation speed
- Toggle planet texture, wings, and weapon display
- Miner whitelist for additional ore items
- Low-fidelity mode for performance optimization

## How to Obtain

### Gilded Blade Wings
Craft at a Workbench with:
- 20 Iron Bars
- 20 Fallen Stars

### Tesseract Staff
Craft at a Workbench with:
- 1 Finch Staff (Item ID: 4281)

### Azafure Miner
Craft at a Heavy Work Bench with:
- 10 Iron Bars

# 耀刃之翼 & 超立方杖

一个具有成长机制、超立方召唤物和数字采矿机的独特模组。

## 主要特性

### 翅膀成长系统
- 翅膀会随着你击败BOSS而变得更强大
- 每击败一个BOSS，飞行时间增加0.5秒
- 水平飞行速度随击败BOSS数量提升
- 每个BOSS提供的额外属性加成：
  - +5 最大生命值
  - +20% 伤害
  - +0.5% 伤害减免
  - +1 防御力
  - +6 生命再生
  - +5 最大魔力
- 每击败5个BOSS：
  - +1 召唤栏
  - +1 哨兵栏

### 超立方杖
- 召唤最多10个虚空分身使用背包物品攻击敌人
- 每个分身占用2个召唤栏，最多同时存在10个
- 分身使用背包40-49号槽位中的物品
- 弹幕从分身位置发射，而非玩家位置
- 独特的立方体线条渲染，支持配置：
  - 散光强度
  - 线条粗细
  - 颜色饱和度
  - 动画速度
  - 可开关星球纹理、翅膀、武器显示
- 攻击力显示为"???"，实际伤害取决于槽位中的物品
- 使用雀杖在工作台合成

### 阿萨福勒数字型采矿机
- 自动挖掘世界中的矿石
- 6个过滤器槽位用于设置要挖掘的矿石类型
- 35个物品存储槽位
- 支持宝石挖掘（紫宝石、黄宝石、蓝宝石、绿宝石、红宝石、钻石）
- 白名单功能可添加自定义矿石或物品
- 粒子效果提供视觉反馈
- 多人游戏中方块与库存数据保持同步

### 配置
- 超立方渲染参数配置：散光、线条、饱和度、动画速度
- 可开关星球纹理、翅膀、武器显示
- 采矿机白名单物品设置
- 低精度模式优化性能

## 获取方式

### 耀刃之翼
在工作台合成：
- 20个铁锭
- 20个落星

### 超立方杖
在工作台合成：
- 1根雀杖（Finch Staff，物品ID：4281）

### 阿萨福勒采矿机
在重型工作台合成：
- 10个铁锭
